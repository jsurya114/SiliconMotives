## Page briefs and internal links

Each commercial page should answer: who this fits, the problem we handle, what the engagement delivers, relevant technical decisions, proof from actual work, how delivery starts, responsibilities/support boundaries, and the next action. Do not invent prices, timelines, specific overlap hours or technical capabilities to complete the brief.

Use the homepage service cards and Services navigation to reach these pages when approved. Service pages should link to relevant existing project URLs and the appropriate enquiry route. Add breadcrumbs and contextual links between related services where useful. The agency page should link to backend, cloud and DevOps support plus relevant project evidence. Project pages should link back to the service they substantiate. Keep key commercial pages within two navigation clicks where practical.

## Validate U.S. demand before finalizing priorities

1. Export U.S., English, Google Search keyword data for the candidate groups from Keyword Planner or the chosen licensed dataset. Record source, retrieval date, geography, match/variant aggregation, volume/ranges, trend and CPC. Difficulty scores require their own source; do not infer them from volume.
2. Inspect a controlled U.S. search sample for each shortlisted group. Record actual result URLs, page types, agency-vs-direct-buyer intent and overlap. Do not label public search-tool ordering as a Google rank.
3. Separate direct-business and agency buying journeys. A high-volume developer tutorial query can be less useful than a smaller commercial partner query.
4. If GSC is available, export aggregate U.S. queries and landing pages over a defined period, separating brand/nonbrand and mobile/desktop. Confirm the property and final hostname first. A newly connected domain may have little history.
5. Choose page order from capability/proof fit, commercial relevance, intent distinctness and measured opportunity. The initial sequence above is based on fit and available evidence, **not** validated U.S. demand.
6. Measure qualified project/agency enquiries rather than promising a traffic percentage or a ranking deadline. Revisit page ownership using real query-to-page evidence; do not declare cannibalization solely because two pages mention AWS.

Recommended launch scope after technical approval: strengthen `/agency-partners`, publish the custom-software and cloud-infrastructure pages, publish one DevOps page, then add backend once its distinct evidence is confirmed. Avoid launching every proposed URL at once with generic text.
