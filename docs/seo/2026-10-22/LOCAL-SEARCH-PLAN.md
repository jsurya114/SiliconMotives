# Website-design visibility: Kochi, Kerala and India

22 October 2026. This adds a local website-design acquisition goal to the engineering/agency strategy. It does not replace that strategy or approve application changes.

## Objective and measurement

Work toward top-10 organic visibility for relevant website-design searches, starting with Kochi. Track each query, search location and device separately. Top-10 visibility for every searcher and across all three geographic scopes cannot be guaranteed. Google cautions against guaranteed-ranking promises. [Google SEO guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/do-i-need-seo)

Treat Kochi as the first target, Kerala as the next scope, and nationwide India as a longer-term opportunity. This sequencing is a recommendation based on the company's actual location and evidence, not a measured keyword-difficulty comparison.

This establishes useful competitor content examples; their search positions and business claims were not independently verified.

The national query sample includes [Clutch](https://clutch.co/in/web-designers) and [The Manifest](https://themanifest.com/in/web-design/agencies), illustrating directory/comparison intent alongside provider intent. SiliconMotives needs evidence that helps a buyer evaluate its work, rather than repeatedly calling itself “best.” These are public search observations, not a controlled Google rank report or quantified demand dataset.
